## Change

- **The poster, a picture's shape and a recording's length**
  (`selenita_core::poster`): `poster_argv(video, out_png)` is
  `gst-launch-1.0 -q filesrc location=<video> ! qtdemux ! decodebin !
  videoconvert ! pngenc snapshot=true ! filesink location=<out_png>`, each
  `location=` one argument built from the path's bytes (the launcher
  escapes a space inside one argument itself, so nothing is quoted and
  nothing goes through a lossy conversion); `png_size` reads a PNG's
  `IHDR`; `mp4_duration` walks the top-level boxes to `moov` → `mvhd`
  (version 0 and 1), every step forward, so a damaged file ends the walk;
  `clock_text` is the recording card's `m:ss`/`h:mm:ss` rule.
- **The `Adopt` rule** (`selenita_core::history::adopted`, `Folders`): a
  regular file (not a link) directly in the captures or the recordings
  folder, `.png` a screenshot and `.mp4` a recording (either case), is an
  entry taken now; anything else is `None`. The captures folder's name is
  product copy, so the caller names both folders.
- **The capture worker** (`capture.rs`, `preview.rs`): after a capture
  saved to a file and after a recording joins the history (`Job::Push`)
  it reports `Report::Preview` with the file, its kind, the poster, the
  shape and the length. A recording's poster runs through the runner
  under `POSTER_DEADLINE` (3 s) into the runtime folder's
  `selenita/poster-<n>.png`; a failed or empty poster leaves no file and
  the preview shows the film glyph; the previous preview's poster is
  removed when a new preview replaces it. `Job::Edit(key)` hands the file
  to Fluorita (`preview::hand_off`): `org.celestina.Fluorita1.Edit(key)`
  when `org.celestina.Fluorita` has an owner, else `fluorita --edit <path>`
  with null standard streams in its own process group, reaped on a thread;
  a bus that cannot be asked counts as no owner; a failure is
  `CaptureError::Edit` («No se ha podido abrir Fluorita.») reported as
  `Report::EditFailed`. `Job::Adopt { key, folder }` applies the rule in
  the pictures folder's captures folder and the videos folder's
  `Recordings` (`record::Places::resolve`, now `pub(crate)`). The backends
  gain `edit_in_fluorita` and `poster`; the fake prints the hand-off and
  writes its 1×1 PNG as the poster. `preview.rs` is the only file with the
  literal `org.celestina.Fluorita1`, as the scanner's allowlist says.
- **The controller**: `previewVisible`, `previewKey` (pathkey),
  `previewFileUri`, `previewKind`, `previewSource` (`QUrl`: the picture or
  the poster), `previewDuration`, `previewAspect`, `previewNotice`;
  `previewShown()`; `dismissPreview()`, `editPreview()` (hides, then
  queues the hand-off) and `adopt(key)`. A `History` report without the
  preview's key hides the preview (a trash). A failed hand-off sets the
  notice and shows the preview again with it, besides the window's
  notice. `launchCapture` is now any key-binding launch (`--screenshot`
  or `--record`, `activation::launch_waiting`); its main window is never
  revealed when the capture ends (the old `reveal` is gone), only on a
  failure whose notice would otherwise not be read: a failed capture, a
  failure after it (`Report::Failed`: the clipboard, the history file) or
  a failed recording (`failure_reveals`).
- **How Selenita ends** (review round 1): the preview is a parentless
  top-level window, so while it showed, closing the main window left Qt
  a visible window and the process lingered with none once the preview
  hid itself (a hide never makes Qt look again), and a recording under way
  never reached `record::quit`. Now the main window's `onClosing` hides
  the preview at once (`hideNow`) and dismisses it, so Qt finds no window
  left and quits as before; `record::quit` finishes a recording. A
  key-binding launch ends by itself: the controller emits
  `launchFinished` once (`launch_over`: a launch, no preview on screen,
  no capture under way, the recording idle) when its preview is
  dismissed or trashed, when a click's hand-off has returned
  (`Report::Edited`), or when a capture ends without a file (a cancelled
  region, a clipboard-only capture); `Main.qml` then hides the preview at
  once and calls `Qt.quit()` unless the main window is on screen. The
  quit asks every visible window to close, which a fading preview
  refused (it turned a compositor close into a dismiss) and which looped
  until both were made idempotent: the signal is said once, the quit
  hides the preview first, and a hidden preview accepts the close.
- **Posters** (review round 1): only in the runtime folder's `selenita`,
  made private (`0700`) through `celestina_core::xdg::ensure_private_dir`;
  with no runtime folder there is no poster (`poster_folder` is `None`),
  never `/tmp/selenita/poster-<n>.png`. The spawned editor's reaper is a
  named thread whose failure to start is logged.
- **`Adopt` on `org.celestina.Selenita1`** (`activation.rs`): a key that
  decodes is handed on (`Request::Adopt`, the `adoptRequested` signal, the
  activation route, `SelenitaController.adopt`); anything else is ignored
  without error. `Request` is `Clone` now (it carries a `String`).
- **`PreviewWindow.qml`**: a second top-level `Window`, `transientParent:
  null`, `title: qsTr("Vista previa")`, `flags: Qt.Window |
  Qt.FramelessWindowHint | Qt.WindowDoesNotAcceptFocus`, transparent with
  the `CelestinaBackdrop`, 240 px wide and 140–240 px high at the shape;
  the picture or poster cropped in a `radiusMd` frame, the film glyph and
  the length in a pill for a recording, the film glyph alone without a
  poster, the × (`CelestinaIconButton`, no focus), the hand-off's notice;
  `HoverHandler`, `DragHandler { target: null }` driving `Drag.active`
  with `Drag.Automatic`, `Drag.supportedActions` and `proposedAction`
  `Qt.CopyAction`, `Drag.mimeData: { "text/uri-list": previewFileUri }`
  and the picture as the drag image; `TapHandler` for the click; a
  `Timer` of 5000 ms that runs only while neither the pointer nor a drag
  holds it; a 200 ms fade and slide, immediate with reduced motion; a
  close from the compositor while it shows is a dismiss; `hideNow` for the
  application's end.
- **The URI list's line ends**: Qt Quick turns a string under
  `text/uri-list` into a `QUrl` and calls `QMimeData::setUrls`, which
  writes each URL followed by CRLF (RFC 2483), and warns «is not a valid
  URI» for one it cannot parse (the warning's text is in the host's
  `libQt6Quick.so.6`); a CRLF added to the string would have become part
  of the URL, so none is added. A live drop is VAL-SEL-PREVIEW's.

Canonical owners: the poster argv and the `Adopt` rule live in
`selenita-core` beside the history and the tool argv (Fluorita's side of
`Adopt` can call the same `adopted`); the Qt seam owns the worker jobs and
the hand-off; QML presents. Searched for an existing PNG-header or MP4
reader (`rg -l "IHDR|mvhd" --type rust`): only `fluorita-core`'s
`artwork.rs`, which walks PNG chunks to add thumbnail keys (another job,
in another application's crate, which `selenita-core` must not depend
on), and test fixtures; no MP4 reader. `clock_text` repeats `RecordingCard.qml`'s
rule in Rust, as the brief fixes `previewDuration` as text; the two are
pinned by their tests.

## Result

- **Exit:** 0 for every check listed, after the fixes below.
- **Crate:** 56 tests pass (46 before: the poster argv with spaces and
  with non-UTF-8 bytes, `png_size`, `mp4_duration` for version 0, version
  1, a size-0 box running to its parent's end (top level and inside
  `moov`), a 64-bit size box stepped over, a 64-bit size under its own
  header refused and seven damaged shapes, `clock_text`, and the `Adopt` rule over a
  scratch home with links, folders, subfolders, other extensions and
  relative paths); clippy clean.
- **Application:** 46 tests pass (35 before): which reports reveal a
  key-binding launch's window (`failure_reveals`: a failed capture and a
  later failure, never a success, a cancel or a hand-off), when a launch
  is over (`launch_over`), the poster folder made `0700` and no poster
  without one; the hand-off over a fake
  editor (`Edit(<key>)` when Fluorita runs; `spawn --edit <path>` when it
  does not or the bus cannot be asked; a failure is `CaptureError::Edit`
  in Spanish), the picture's preview and shape, a recording's fake poster
  and its replacement (the old file removed), a failed poster that leaves
  no file and no picture, `Adopt` joining once and ignoring the rest with
  the history file written, a vanished file not handed off, and `Adopt`
  on the bus handing on only keys that decode.
- **QML:** 64 test functions pass three times in a row (46 before); the
  preview's 13: the title `Vista previa`, frameless and
  `WindowDoesNotAcceptFocus`, no transient parent, hidden until a result;
  appears on `previewShown` with the 5000 ms timer running; still there at
  4.5 s and gone by 6 s with one `dismissPreview`; the pointer stops the
  timer and leaving restarts it; the × dismisses (and the card's tap does
  not fire); a click calls `editPreview` and closes; a recording replaces
  a picture (film glyph, `0:07`, the 140 px floor of a 16:9 shape); no
  poster shows the film glyph alone; the drag's `text/uri-list`, copy
  action and `Drag.Automatic`; a trash closes it without a dismiss; reduced
  motion shows and hides at once; motion fades in; the hand-off's notice.
  `tst_main`: a capture from the window shows the preview and keeps the
  window; a key-binding launch (`launchCapture`) shows only the preview,
  for a capture and a recording, and a failure shows the window; closing
  the window with the preview on screen hides the preview at once and
  dismisses it; `Adopt` reaches the controller.
- **Smoke:** both report runs say `preview=shown`; the plain launch ends
  `shown=true` and still runs at 8 s (124); the `--screenshot screen`
  launch starts and ends `shown=false` and, once its preview is dismissed
  at 5 s, ends by itself (0); the fake recording's poster is the one
  `run/selenita/poster-*.png`. The third run closes the window at 2 s
  with the capture's preview on screen and a fake recording under way:
  the process ends (0) with the PNG, the MP4 and both history lines. With
  the `onClosing` fix taken out of `Main.qml` the same run ends at the
  timeout (124), so it catches the lingering process. Offscreen the
  pointer rests at (0, 0), on the preview, and holds its timer for good
  (traced: `hovered true`, `holding false` every second), which is why
  the launch run dismisses the preview as its × does rather than waiting
  for the five seconds; the timer itself is the QML tests'.
- **Real poster:** the synthetic MP4 (320×180, 2 s) gave a 320×180 RGBA
  PNG in 0.04 s; `extract_poster` returned `Ok` in 36 ms and `preview::of`
  with the real backend gave `aspect 0.5625` and `length 2s` (the same as
  `gst-discoverer-1.0`'s `0:00:02.000000000`); a missing video fails with
  exit 255 and leaves no poster.
- **qmllint:** selenita stays at 1 (the baseline `CelestinaIcons`
  singleton note). A first `previewSource.toString()` raised it to 2 and
  was replaced by the picture's `status`.
- **Radius:** the first notice box (radiusSm inside radiusMd at spaceSm)
  broke the concentric rule; it sits at spaceXs now (8 + 4 = 12).
- **Offscreen focus:** the offscreen platform activates every window it
  shows, `WindowDoesNotAcceptFocus` or not, so a preview after every stub
  capture took the keyboard focus from the main window in `tst_keyboard`;
  the stand-in controller shows the preview on a result only when a test
  asks (`previewOnResult`). On the session niri's rule (`open-focused
  false`) keeps the focus, which VAL-SEL-PREVIEW checks.
- **Pointer events in the tests:** a click sent before the preview's
  first frame was lost; the tests wait for the frame and move the pointer
  off the preview before acting (a pointer left over the same spot by a
  previous test held it).

## Limits

- Nothing here proves the placement, the focus or a drop into another
  program under niri and Wayland (the window rule, `Drag.Automatic`'s
  `wl_data_device` offer, WhatsApp's and Slack's acceptance of the URI),
  nor Fluorita's `Edit` (FLU-P1-A lands in parallel): VAL-SEL-PREVIEW.
- The real hand-off (`preview::Session`) was not run against a bus: the
  smoke has none and an agent does not start Fluorita on the session; its
  decision is tested over the fake editor.
- The real poster ran on a synthetic file, not on a recording of the
  session; a VA-API-encoded recording needs a decoder `decodebin` finds
  (`gst-libav` or `gst-plugin-va`), else the film glyph shows.
- `scripts/complete-production.sh` is the landing's, not this session's.
