## Decisions

- No shader exists in `celestina-style`, so the reading shader is local
  (`calcita/qml/shaders/`), compiled by `qsb` at build time as Qt 6 expects;
  `QSB` overrides the baker's path, else it is found beside `qmake6`.
- The effect is the page view's `layer.effect`: the bar, the cards and the
  scroll bar stay as drawn; the hits and the selection are inverted with
  the pages. The layer exists only while the amount is above 0, so reading
  as drawn costs nothing.
- The shader un-premultiplies before inverting, so the gaps between pages
  stay clear over the window's glass; the hue turn is the
  luminance-preserving 180° rotation, so white maps to black and black to
  white exactly.
- The store keeps the mode as a fifth field (`dark`/`light`); a line
  without it, written by CAL-1-A or CAL-1-B, reads as `light`.
- The row menu opens with a right click, the Menu key or Shift+F10, beside
  the row (`popupBeside`); the menu is a `GlassContextMenu` over the
  window's content.
- Accessibility: the bar is a named tool bar; the page field's name is
  the page and the count («n de N») with a description of its grammar; the zoom label «Zoom n %», the
  reading button «Lectura oscura»/«Lectura clara» as a checkable control;
  the search card and the outline card are named panes; a nested outline
  row says its level; a recent row reads «Abrir <name>»; the notice and the
  link pill already announced themselves. Calcita has no spinner; the
  notice, the link pill and the menu already honour `reducedMotion`.
- The appearance follower is the shared one (`celestina_settings::follow`,
  first value from `celestina_settings::load()`), unchanged; reduced motion
  reaches the reading fade through `CelestinaTheme.reducedMotion`.

## Limits

- The offscreen platform renders with Qt Quick's software backend, which
  has no shader effects: the tests prove the toggling, the fade and the
  persistence, not the pixels. How the inverted pages look is
  VAL-CAL-DARK in [VALIDATION.md](../../VALIDATION.md), with the handler
  pinned by hand in `mimeapps.list`.
- A recent forgotten before the store finished its first read can come
  back from the file when the read arrives (the menu only shows once the
  list is there, so this needs a document closed in that first moment).
- The reading mode shares the page's and the zoom's races: a toggle before
  the document is Ready is not remembered (`remember` waits for the load),
  and a document opened before the store's first read restores `light`.
- `HOST-HYGIENE.md` is the suite's exit (Task 9), not this unit's.
- `scripts/complete-production.sh` runs at the landing, not here.

## Landing fix: the outline under load

The landing's verify failed `Outline::test_f9_shows_the_two_bookmarks` and
`test_enter_follows_the_current_bookmark` (`rowCount` stayed 0) while
another release build loaded the CPU. Reproduced with a busy loop on every
core (2 failures in 6 runs, 1 in 12). Logged at the failure: the pooled
`PdfDocument` was Ready with 3 pages and QtPdf's `PdfBookmarkModel` had 0
rows after 5 s, so the model missed the Ready of the document's new load.
Setting its `document` to null to rebind it crashes QtPdf (a segfault in
`QQuickPdfBookmarkModel`), so `OutlinePanel` now makes a fresh bookmark
model on every Ready of its document (and drops it otherwise). The tests
wait for the document's status and give `rowCount` 15 s as a second guard.
After the fix: 15 of 15 runs under the same load and the normal run pass
(84 each); qmllint stays at the baseline; smoke, the release build and the
contracts exit 0.
