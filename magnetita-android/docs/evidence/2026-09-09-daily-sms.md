## Design

- `Messages` reads the provider: the conversation list is the newest
  message of each thread with its unread count; a thread page is the
  newest fifty (or those older than the desktop's mark), oldest first. A
  send goes through the system's SMS manager in parts, and the desktop's
  thread gets the sent message back over the wire, since a non-default
  app cannot write the provider.
- `Messages.Receiver` takes the system's incoming-SMS broadcast, joins the
  parts, finds or creates the thread and sends the message as received.

## Procedure

```sh
cd magnetita-android
./gradlew --no-daemon -q assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest lintRelease
adb shell pm grant org.celestina.magnetita android.permission.READ_SMS
busctl --user --json=short call … SmsConversations s 4eb6f9984054dd25
```

## Result

- **Exit:** 0. The signals test pins the three requests and the send; the
  controller test sends a received message in order.
- **On the S25U:** the conversation list went out on the session's
  request and the desktop lists it.

## Limits

- MMS parts are not read; attachments stay counted.
- Sending to and receiving from a real correspondent is `VAL-MAG-12`.
