## Hypothesis

Each daily capability is one Kotlin adapter between a platform service and
the core's session; the policies that decide what travels are pure Kotlin
with JVM tests, and the protocol stays in Rust.

## Tangible outcome

The phone half of the author's daily set: clipboard, notifications, files,
media, contacts, SMS and calls, each verified on the S25U against the daemon.

## Scope

- `AND-2-A` — clipboard: the desktop's text becomes the phone's; the
  phone's goes out while the application is in front, from the
  quick-settings tile through a focus-holding window, and from the share
  target; a policy refuses echoes, blanks and oversize text.
- `AND-2-B` — notifications with actions and replies.
- `AND-2-C` — file share both ways.
- `AND-2-D` — media control both ways.
- `AND-2-E` — contacts sync.
- `AND-2-F` — SMS.
- `AND-2-G` — telephony.

## Exclusions

- The Rust side of each capability: `MAG-P4`.
- Commands, input and mirror.

## Build order

1. `AND-2-A` through `-D` as their `MAG-P4` units land; `-E` before `-F`
   and `-G`.

## Implementation exit

Every adapter has its JVM tests, `lintRelease` passes, and each capability
is observed on the S25U against the daemon.
