## Hypothesis

Every systemd unit of the session and the system can be listed on the
sampler thread from both buses, the user's own units started and stopped
without authorisation, and every privileged action reported truthfully,
including the absence of an agent.

## Tangible outcome

The installed 0.6.0 has a Services page listing every user and system unit,
lets the person start, stop and restart their own units freely and a
system unit or a foreign process through polkit, and reads the outcome of
every privileged action as a Spanish sentence, including a missing
authentication agent.

## Scope

- `H5-A` — `hematita-core`: `services`, the unit projection and the
  privileged-action outcome mapping.
- `H5-B` — sampler services section, `HematitaServices`, the Services page,
  and the privileged actions themselves (`StartUnit`/`StopUnit`/
  `RestartUnit` over both buses, `pkexec kill` for a foreign process).
- `H5-Z` — implementation exit and 0.6.0.

## Exclusions

- A polkit agent; unit files not loaded (`ListUnitFiles`); the journal;
  timers' schedules; editing units.

## Build order

1. `H5-A`, then `H5-B`, `H5-Z`.

## Implementation exit

`scripts/complete-production.sh` succeeds; the installed binary lists every
user and system unit, manages the user's own freely and a system unit or a
foreign process through polkit, and reports every privileged outcome
truthfully.
