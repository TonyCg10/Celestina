## Hypothesis

A pure Rust shell over smithay-client-toolkit, femtovg, cosmic-text and
taffy, with the compositor supplying the glass, can give the author's niri
session an Island that grows into pages, a launcher, notifications and a tray
at the frame, memory and rest budgets the spike measured.

## Tangible outcome

One process per session draws an Island capsule on the top edge of every
output that grows into the Activity, Notifications and Tray pages; a launcher
overlay ranks the session's desktop entries; banners show notifications with
actions; the tray hosts StatusNotifierItems with DBusMenu popups; the binary
also forwards the command verbs of `org.celestina.Shell1`.

## Scope

- The three toolkit crates, the Island capsule and the command surface
  (CEL-1-B).
- The Island pages, the launcher, the notifications server and the tray
  (CEL-1-D to CEL-1-G), and the exit (CEL-1-H).

## Exclusions

- The shared tokens (CEL-1-C), delivered by `celestina-style` and
  `celestina-rs` in their own ledgers.
- Parkable pieces, the full bar, OSD, media, clipboard history, lock, polkit,
  other schemes, wallpaper and accessibility through `accesskit`: later
  deliveries with their own designs.
- A dock, plugins, scripting and any compositor other than niri, for good.

## Build order

1. CEL-1-B: the workspace, the toolkit crates and one capsule per output.
2. CEL-1-C (outside this ledger): tokens as data; the shell switches to the
   crate.
3. CEL-1-D: the Island pages.
4. CEL-1-E: the launcher.
5. CEL-1-F: notifications.
6. CEL-1-G: the tray.
7. CEL-1-H: the exit and 1.0.0.

## Implementation exit

`celestina/scripts/verify-production.sh` passes on the CEL-1-H tree (format,
clippy with warnings denied, the workspace tests including the pure
`IslandState`, `celestina-ui` and niri-parser tests, and the smoke over a
nested niri), and every live row above is queued in `VALIDATION.md`.
