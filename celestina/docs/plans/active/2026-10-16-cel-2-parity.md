# CEL-2 — Shell parity with Noctalia

- **Opened:** 2026-10-16
- **Plan ID:** cel-2-parity
- **Status:** active
- **Authorization:** the author approved the program's design on 2026-10-16
  ([the design](../../../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-celestina-parity-program-design.md));
  the program is opened by the suite unit `CEL-2-OPEN` of the RSH-1 ledger, which
  also archives the CEL-1 plan
- **Scope:** celestina
- **Implementation checkpoint:** CEL-2
- **Author-validation checkpoint:** VAL-CEL2-BAR-LIVE in [VALIDATION.md](../../../VALIDATION.md) (it does not block)

## Hypothesis

A Rust shell with the piece contract of the design can take over, duty by
duty and beside a running Noctalia, everything Noctalia does in the author's
niri session, so that Noctalia can be stopped after the cutover unit.

## Tangible outcome

The full-width bar with zones and the Island in its centre, and the duties of
the design's §5 (energy and comfort, OSD, media, wallpaper, connectivity,
clipboard, lock, polkit, phone and bubbles, glance, the desktop overview),
each switched on by the author through `shell.json`.

## Scope

- The thirteen units CEL-2-A to CEL-2-M of the design, in its order.

## Exclusions

- Style, animation and UX polish (group 2), a dock, plugins and scripting,
  AI, any compositor but niri, and the Noctalia plugins the design lists.

## Build order

1. CEL-2-A: the bar and the piece contract.
2. CEL-2-B and CEL-2-E in parallel with A where the author runs them so;
   then C, D, F, G, J, K and L on A's contract; H and I before M.
3. CEL-2-M: the cutover.

## Implementation exit

Every unit's row is `done` and `celestina/scripts/verify-production.sh`
passes on the CEL-2-M tree; the live rows are queued in `VALIDATION.md`.
