## Change

- **Matcher:** `celestina_shell::launcher_search` prepares each desktop entry once per index:
  - both names, localized and not;
  - the generic names, the keywords and Spanish category aliases;
  - `Exec`.
  
  It scores a query's terms in any order: 60 for an equal name, 50 for a name prefix, 40 for a name word start, 30 for a secondary word start, 20 or 15 for one edit (terms of 4 characters or more), and 10 or 5 for letters in order. An entry's score is the mean of its terms; a query equal to the display name scores 60. At most 8 terms of 64 characters are read.
- **Use record:** `launcher::usage` keeps `usage.json` with each application's launch count and last launch. The score is the count times a weight by age: 100, 70, 50, 30 or 10. The record is bounded to 200 applications, and the application just launched is never the one dropped (otherwise a new application could never enter a full record). It is seeded once from `recent.json`, which is no longer written.
- **Groups and aliases:** `launcher::groups` assigns each application to one of seven groups by the design's priority, and gives each its Spanish category words (copy constants `LAUNCHER_GROUP_*` and `LAUNCHER_ALIAS_*`).
- **Launcher:** rows show the localized `Name` and `GenericName` when the file has them. An empty query lists every application by use score, then name. The `/` switches and the `Mod+K` selector are unchanged. `MAX_ROWS` (8) still caps the rows until `CEL-I-017`.

  - fixed: a new index that kept every id but changed a name was never prepared again, so a rename or a translation showed only after a restart;
  - deferred: a Flatpak entry's long `Exec` lets short queries match it at the lowest tier, which the old matcher also did (`CEL-I-017` lifts the row cap, so the tail will show it);
  - kept, as designed: the letters-in-order tier that brings the Zelda game into the `imagenes` search.

## Limits

- **Flatpak in the nest.** The nest starts from a clean environment and does not see the author's Flatpak applications (Floorp, LibreWolf, Steam). That is why the browser search shows only Zen, which is not a Flatpak; `VAL-CEL-I-015` checks the three browsers in the author's session.
- **Use order.** It was checked by tests, not in the nest.
