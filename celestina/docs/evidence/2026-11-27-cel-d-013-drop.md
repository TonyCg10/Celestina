Frames. `gpu-screen-recorder` would record the host's screen, not the nest, so a burst is a loop of `grim -t ppm -g REGION` on the nest's output, a 400 by 300 logical box at the end capsule (the whole top strip for the cross-capsule bursts). With a crop, `grim` takes about 7.5 ms a shot, so a burst gives a shot about every 8 ms and every distinct frame the shell drew. The loop logs the start and end of every shot (the instant of a frame is taken as their midpoint, about 4 ms of doubt), and the virtual pointer helper logs the epoch time of each press just before it sends it, so every tile is labelled with its offset from the press. Time from the press to the first changed pixel under the capsule was +107 ms on an open and +73 ms on a close (energy), +95 and +74 ms (network): that is the nest's input and frame latency, not part of the animation. The last changing frame came at +463 ms (energy close) and +460 ms (network close), so the close takes about 390 ms from its first visible frame. The session read every contact sheet below; tiles carry `+N ms #shot`.

Scenarios (one burst each unless said): open and close of the energy menu and of the network menu; a switch from energy to network 340 ms after the open's press (in the OutBack), 120 ms (the swell) and 200 ms (the fall); a press on the bell (another capsule) 220 ms and 330 ms after the energy press; the same piece pressed again 150 ms after its close began; reduced motion (`appearance.toml` with `reduced_motion = true`, picked up live) open and close; the energy menu at scale 1.25.

## What the author decided and how the plan changed

- **G1 turned down.** 2026-11-27, after seeing G1 in the nest (the rounded rectangle plus neck plus tabs plus tips plus fillets): (translated from the author's Spanish) "I don't like the style, I want it to look more like a drop, it looks like a ball with odd bumps".
- **D1 chosen.** The same day the author picked D1, "hanging drop", of preview v4: one continuous liquid contour from a half-width profile mirrored on the piece's centre, a round bulb of radius 12, a thread that pinches near the bulb on open, and on close the window gathering into the bulb, which rises, meets a cone the capsule reaches down, merges and is absorbed. Tasks 1.2b and 1.3b ported it; the nest gate (Task 1.5b) was approved by the author the same day ("yes, I approve", translated). The gate shots are in [d1](2026-11-27-cel-d-013-drop/d1/) and the G1 ones in [g1-rejected](2026-11-27-cel-d-013-drop/g1-rejected/).
- **The plan amendment** (`DropFrame` carries `liquid: Option<Liquid>` and `drawn: bool` instead of a join; `MERGE_AT` is 0.8, so the pill returns at 320 ms of the close; `Source.w` is kept and unused) lives in the working notes, not in the repository. The design and plan in `docs/superpowers` still describe G1 (the rounded rectangle, `Join`, `MERGE_AT` 0.78, 312 ms). A docs follow-up is needed.
- **Rulings taken during the unit** (see the ledger notes): a joined capsule is left out of the blur band set and its path holds it; a window joins its capsule's path only while the capsule's opacity is 1; the "no step" and "absorbs" tests use the preview's own profile (vertical tangents and a hidden sliver at the capsule edge); the piece is held until `merged` (319 ms held, 320 ms released) and under reduced motion released at the close's start.
- **Parked from Task 5, seen in the nest, fixed by round 1 of the final review (below):** a window leaving on another capsule did not hold its piece (the old pill faded in 150 ms while its drop returned over 400 ms); and a travel from a drawn frame snapped the radius to 20.

## The femtovg answers (Task 1.4, femtovg 0.27.0, from the source)

- A fill rule exists: `Paint.fill_rule`, default `NonZero`; the shell sets it.
- No winding is forced: a contour is reversed only if it carries a solid or hole marker, and the path builder emits none. The stencil counts front faces up and back faces down and the cover pass tests not-equal-zero, so two overlapping clockwise contours fill as their union.
- Fringes lie outside the fill. This was read from the source and then confirmed on screen by Task 1.5b (below).

## Two paint findings, and their fixes (Tasks 1.5b and 1.6)

1. **Pointed 1 px nubs at the equator of every rounded end** (the capsule's and the bulb's). Cause: `ShapePath::rounded_rect` left a zero-length `line_to` on a side when the height is twice the radius, and femtovg's fringe spikes there. Fix: segments under 0.01 px are skipped. Confirmed in the nest: in the final build the capsule's right end and the bulb's sides are clean ([ends at x8](2026-11-27-cel-d-013-drop/nest-d1/ends-x8.jpg), [bulb at x8](2026-11-27-cel-d-013-drop/nest-d1/bulb-x8.jpg); left: no menu, then two joined frames).
2. **The path fill is about one device pixel fatter than the tree's rounded rect** (a 1 to 2 px step on the capsule's top edge in the first gate shots). Cause: femtovg gives a single convex contour half a fringe and every other path, a union of several contours, a full fringe. Fix: a joined path is built inset by 0.5 device pixel. Checked in the nest by comparing a no-menu frame with joined frames (the swell, the bulb, the thread) column by column across the capsule: the flat part of the top and bottom edges is identical, row for row. On the rounded end (30 columns at the right end) the joined path is still one physical pixel taller at the extreme top and bottom (bottom row 72 against 71 at scale 1.5; a few columns also one row higher at the top). That is about 0.7 logical pixel on the curve only, visible at x8 in `ends-x8.jpg`; it is left as is.

## Limits

- The shell hard-codes the dark theme: there is no light theme to shoot, so every shot is dark. `VAL-CEL-D-013` has no theme step for that reason.
- The nest has no tray item, so there is no tray chevron window; the network menu is the second menu.
- The nest has no brightness piece (`CELESTINA_BACKLIGHT=0`).
- The shots show the shell's own glass over the nest's wallpaper (the nest's wallpaper). niri's blur behind the window and the neck is only seen in the author's real session: `VAL-CEL-D-013`.
- Only the dark wallpaper was used. The earlier gates also shot a light busy window behind (Task 1.5b) with no halo seen; it was not repeated here.
- The nest's input and frame latency (about 70 to 110 ms) means the burst's frame offsets are from the press, not from the animation's start; the 460 ms and 400 ms curves are pinned by the clock tests.
- The monitors (three real outputs, different scales), the wheel, right click and a press on the open piece were not driven.
- `cargo +1.100.0` is not installed here; clippy for that toolchain ran through `rustup run 1.100.0 cargo clippy` in the earlier tasks.

## Final review

Round 1 of the final review (Opus, high; `code-review high` plus a read of the code) found 0 Critical, 1 Important and 8 Minor, 5 Nit. Nothing in it was run in the nest or in the author's session. Round 1 fixed these, each with a test that failed before the fix:

- **M7, the liquid's top past a rounded capsule end** (the author turned G1 down for "odd bumps"). `Liquid` has `edge`, the x-range of the capsule's flat bottom. Round 1 held each side of the contour to the room left on that flat bottom; round 2 found that this leaves a lopsided root with a 45° corner on the default layout's last piece (its centre 29 px from the end). After the last round the session replaced it: the root (the thread's `a` and its fillet) narrows on both sides alike to that room, keeping up to half of it for the fillet, so the outline is the same smooth profile, only thinner near an end. A piece in mid-capsule keeps its contour. Tests cover a piece 28 and 29 px from either end (symmetric, no step from 1 px under the edge, nothing above the edge past the curve) and a capsule of one 56 px icon.
- **M6, the inset rule.** Only a path of exactly one rounded rect is built without the inset; a liquid alone (one concave contour) is inset like the joined ones.
- **M8 and M4, the window radius.** `open_frame` takes the theme's radius. A close and a travel start from the radius shown (`close_frame` takes `from_radius`; `SheetMotion::close` and `travel` carry it) instead of 20, so a close or a switch from a window mid-open has no corner pop.
- **M1, reduced motion.** A close that cuts the open's fade starts from the glass and content shown (a close at +75 ms of the open starts at 0.5, never 1).
- **M2, the blur region under reduced motion.** An unjoined window path with opacity under 0.35 (`BLUR_REGION_MIN_GLASS`, kept for reduced motion only) is out of the region; joined paths are whole and always in it.
- **N1.** An unjoined drop's liquid starts at the edge, not 2 px above it, so it does not overlap the capsule glass the tree paints.
- **N2.** A press on a frame that is only liquid (the swell, the fall, the close's rise) is a press outside: it takes nothing and, away from the bar and the Island, closes the sheet.
- **N3.** The stale step in the test message ("+260 ms") now says +400 ms.
- **I1, the leaving window's piece.** `Sheets::held_pieces(now)` lists the open sheet's piece, then each leaving window's piece until its own merge (320 ms of its close; none under reduced motion). The bar keeps the 80 % opacity and a whole pill on each held piece, with no slide, and gives each back at its merge on the existing exit fade and 200 ms return. It is still one pill per zone: `CEL-D-014`'s look does not change.

Parked by the controller: M3 (a close during the swell or the fall drops the liquid in one frame), M5 (a switch from a falling bulb jumps to about 73 by 58 px in its first frame), N4 (the glass paths are built twice per frame), N5 (the inset narrows the bulb's widths but not its height). The 1 px taller capsule end while joined also stays.

Round 2 (Opus, high) reread only round 1's fixes and checked each test fails with its fix undone: 0 Critical, 1 Important (the M7 corner above, fixed by the session after the round), 1 Minor and 5 Nit. Parked from round 2: R2-M1 (when a held piece is released while the pointer is back on it, its hover fill shows over the fading pill for 150 ms) and the nits.

After the rounds the session checked the drop at the capsules' rounded ends in the nest, with the same kit: the last piece of the end capsule (power, its centre 28 px from the end) and the bell at the start capsule's right end. With round 1's per-side cut nothing poked past the curve, but the root leaned to one side.
