## What was built

- **`Field` (celestina-ui).** A single-line editor: a string, a byte cursor, a
  selection anchor and a preedit. It is font-free by ruling: the interface
  `edit(&mut self, op)` has no font system, every `cosmic_text::Editor` action
  needs one, and `Editor` deletes one `char` (not a grapheme) and moves by
  shaping cluster, which fails combining accents and flags. Graphemes and words
  come from `unicode-segmentation` (UAX 29, the segmentation `Editor` uses).
  Geometry comes from the shaped `TextRun`, which gained `caret_x`, `index_at`
  and `span`. Text is bounded to 256 characters; Insert, Paste, Commit and
  Preedit strip control characters and cut at a grapheme boundary, and the
  input is bounded before processing. Reconciliation keeps the field while its
  key and `FieldId` survive.
- **IME (celestina-wayland).** `zwp_text_input_v3`: `Preedit`, `CommitText`,
  enable and disable following enter and leave, and the caret rectangle. The
  `done` rule: it applies when the serial falls inside the current `enable`
  (from the commit that carried `enable` up to our latest commit, wrapping). A
  serial from before the current enable, one from the future, or any `done`
  while disabled applies nothing. This is deliberately not "only the matching
  serial": the protocol text says a lagging serial must still be applied except
  for the current state, and dropping it would lose characters whenever a
  caret-rect commit is in flight. Preedit and commit are cut to 1024 bytes at a
  character boundary; `delete_surrounding_text` is ignored.
- **Paste (celestina-wayland).** Clipboard selection only; drag offers are
  refused. One read in flight (a second request is dropped), polled in 50 ms
  slices with a 2 s deadline, a stop flag and a 64 KiB cap. Over the cap the
  text is cut at 64 KiB and a character split by the cut is dropped; invalid
  UTF-8 refuses the whole paste; empty text, a timeout or a cancel at exit
  deliver nothing. The worker is joined when its result arrives and at
  shutdown.
- **Launcher (`src/launcher/`).** A `celestina-overlay` layer surface on the
  output of the keyboard or niri-focused Island, `Layer::Overlay`, exclusive
  keyboard, anchored to all four edges with exclusive zone -1, so it fills the
  whole output (the Island's band included) and pushes no window. The 560 by
  400 logical px card (the Haze card, a pill field and up to eight rows,
  `MAX_ROWS`) is centred inside it on whole pixels. The input region is the
  whole surface; the blur region and the painted silhouette are the card
  only, and the rest is fully transparent, with no dim. Escape, Enter, a row
  click, a press anywhere outside the card, focus loss and the toggle close
  it; every close turns text input off and the app destroys the surface
  through `Message::LauncherClosed`. The runtime gained
  `Anchoring::{Top, Centred, Fill}`: a filling surface asks for 0 by 0 and
  takes its size from the configure.
- **State and ranking.** The salvaged `celestina_shell::launcher::rank` (exact,
  prefix, word start, loose subsequence; ties by name) followed by a stable
  sort by (tier, recency), then cut to eight. Recency lifts an entry within its
  tier only; with an empty query the recent entries lead. The recency rule is
  new: the halted launcher had none.
- **Slash switches.** `/` lists Notificaciones, Actividad, Bandeja and No
  molestar by folded prefix; an unknown command shows no rows and Enter does
  nothing. The first three open an Island page; do-not-disturb flips the
  existing setting through the shared `App::set_do_not_disturb`, refused while
  another program holds the notifications name.
- **recent.json.** `$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/celestina/recent.json`, a JSON array of at
  most 50 ids, newest first; read once at start bounded to 64 KiB, hostile ids
  dropped, a bad file starts empty; written with `atomic_file::replace_private`
  after each launch.
- **Spawn.** `setsid -f` plus, for `Terminal=true`, `kitty -e` (as the halted
  launcher did), plus `exec_argv(entry)`; stdio null, cwd `$HOME`, the
  environment inherited; the short-lived child is reaped on a detached thread.
- **Desktop index.** A worker scans the application directories at start and
  every five minutes with a cancellation token, keeps listable entries that
  show in the current desktop, and posts the list only when it changed.
- **Toolkit changes outside the unit's named files.** `focus_ring` and
  `FOCUS_WIDTH` are public in `celestina-ui`; a focused `Field` draws no focus
  ring because its caret marks the focus.

## Limits

[VALIDATION.md](../../VALIDATION.md). `build-production.sh`,

## Open risks

- While the launcher is open it takes every press on its output: a click
  outside the card closes it and is not passed to the window under it, so
  that click does nothing else. This is by design.
- The launcher's buffer is the whole output: 3840 by 2160 px on the 4K output
  at scale 1.5 (about 32 MiB per buffer, and the EGL swap chain keeps two or
  three) while it is open; it is freed when the surface is destroyed on close.
  An idle launcher draws no frame (frame pacing is unchanged), and a frame
  clears the buffer and paints only the card: the transparent root has no
  fill. The swap carries no damage region, so the compositor treats the whole
  buffer as damaged on each frame (typing, selection).
- `recent.json` is written on the loop thread (a few hundred bytes plus an
  fsync) after each launch, like the do-not-disturb setting.
- `app.rs` is 875 lines after about 250 lines of launcher wiring; the
  architecture guard passes, and it is a candidate for extraction if it keeps
  growing.
- There is no live IME or clipboard evidence (see Limits).

## Rulings and known limitations

- `Row::App` and `LauncherAction::Launch` box the `DesktopEntry` because clippy's
  `large_enum_variant` fails under `-D warnings`.
- A terminal application runs through the hard-coded `kitty -e`, as the halted
  launcher did.
- The caret does not blink; the selection alpha (0.35) and the caret width (1.5)
  are local constants because no theme token exists for them.
- Cursor and selection are byte offsets into the field's text. A pointer press
  mid-composition cancels it. A field keeps its text across focus loss, so the
  launcher clears it on open.
- Text-input and clipboard objects are tied to the keyboard capability and one
  seat is assumed, like the rest of the runtime. Rect and enable requests are
  sent at once rather than held for a `done`.
- Ctrl+V is not consumed by the field; the launcher asks for the paste.
