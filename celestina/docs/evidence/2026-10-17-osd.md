## What was built

- **The duty.** `shell.json` gains `duties.osd`, `off` by default, and an `osd`
  table (`timeout_ms`, default 1500 and bounded to 500..5000; `position`,
  `bottom` or `bottom-right`; `kinds.volume|microphone|brightness`, each true).
  Turning the duty on spawns the workers and creates nothing visible; turning
  it off stops the child, joins the workers and destroys any OSD surface. The
  shell follows the file live.
- **Audio.** One worker observes `pactl subscribe` (bounded 4 KiB lines, child
  killed and reaped on stop) and reads `wpctl get-volume` with the salvaged
  exact-text parser; events are settled 30 ms; if the child dies the worker
  polls every 2 s and respawns it with a growing backoff. The first reading of
  each device is a baseline, never an OSD.
- **Brightness.** sysfs backlights are observed with `poll(POLLPRI)` where they
  exist; otherwise DDC runs on one thread with a `flock` lease, a 20 s timeout
  per operation, a pending target per monitor (newest wins) and a bounded
  queue. DDC is lazy: no `detect` before the duty is on and a brightness
  consumer exists. With the `brightness` piece in a layout the consumer
  exists from the start, so one `detect` runs at start (and at most one
  follow-up, no sooner than 5 s after it, for outputs announced meanwhile);
  without the piece the first brightness verb or popup detects. The worker is
  asked to detect only when a consumer appears; steps and verbs are changes
  only. A re-detection asked by outputs waits 5 s after the last detection, one
  asked again after finding nothing waits 30 s (`REDETECT`). The first write to
  a monitor of unknown level is preceded by a published read, the display's
  baseline. `CELESTINA_DDC=0|false` forbids every `ddcutil` call. There is no
  periodic DDC read.
- **The surface.** `celestina-osd` on `Layer::Overlay`, no keyboard, empty
  input region, anchored bottom-centre (or bottom-right), on the focused
  output, created on show and destroyed after its exit, so rest costs no
  surface and no frame.
- **Verbs.** `volume up|down|mute|set N`, `microphone mute|set N`,
  `brightness up|down|set N [output]`. Step 5, ceiling 100. With the duty off
  they are rejected and the journal says why.
- **Pieces.** `volume` and `brightness` in the bar's end zone with a slider
  popup (pointer, arrows, Home and End). The popup suppresses the display per
  kind (volume and microphone, or brightness), and after it closes the last
  level it asked of each kind is not announced when it is read back (at most
  1 s for audio, 6 s for brightness).
- **Not delivered:** the lock-keys OSD (Noctalia has it off; it would need a
  poll of `/sys/class/leds` or evdev access).

## Limits

Nothing was checked in a real session. Not exercised and not coverable here:

- **No real DDC hardware.** `CELESTINA_DDC=0` and fake `ddcutil` in every
  automated run. The detection output, the read-back, the latency and the
  monitor's response are the author's row VAL-CEL2-OSD-BRIGHTNESS.
- **GPU loss with concurrent `ddcutil`.** The halted shell lost the GPU twice
  with `ddcutil` children on one bus. The shell serializes its own children
  with a lease, but Noctalia also runs `ddcutil`, and no lock inside this
  shell can cover another program's processes. The only mitigation is the
  author's procedure: Noctalia's OSD and brightness widget off while this
  duty is tried. This risk is not closed by any test.
- **No test in the live session.** The real `wpctl`, PipeWire event stream,
  `niri msg layers` for the surface, the focused-output choice on two
  outputs of different scale, the look of the OSD and the popup at 1.5, and
  keys held down are rows VAL-CEL2-OSD-AUDIO and VAL-CEL2-OSD-PIECES.
- **The sysfs path** is unit-tested only: this machine has no
  `/sys/class/backlight` entry.
- **Which backlight an internal output uses** is the first device in
  alphabetical order (`acpi_video0` before `intel_backlight` on a laptop that
  exposes both, where the firmware one is often the wrong one). There is no
  such hardware here to choose a rule against; a laptop row would have to.
- **A lease released with a child not reaped.** When the duty goes off (or
  the shell exits) while a killed `ddcutil` child could not be reaped within
  its grace, the worker logs it and ends, and the session lease it held is
  released with that child possibly still alive: another holder could then
  start a second `ddcutil` on the bus. Not exercised; it needs a child that
  ignores `SIGKILL` for over 2 s.
- **The monitor's own buttons** are not observed (no periodic DDC read); a
  documented gap.
- **Lock-keys OSD: not delivered.** Noctalia has it off and no need was
  stated.
- **Keys keep running `wpctl` and `brightnessctl` directly** until CEL-2-M
  rebinds them; the OSD observes the change after the fact.
- The smoke's frame count at rest is read from the debug log, not from
  `--metrics`: the OSD surface is destroyed before the metrics line is
  printed, so the rest check is the checkpoint ordering described above.

## Open risks

- The review pass (T8) ran; its findings A to H were fixed in the pass
  recorded above. The OSD's behaviour around the popup's last write rests on
  bounded settle windows (1 s audio, 6 s brightness), not on a
  confirmation from the device: a DDC read-back that differs from the level
  asked (a monitor whose range is not 0..100) keeps brightness quiet for the
  whole 6 s.
- The salvage list line of the root `AGENTS.md` for CEL-2-C must be on `main`
  before the unit's first commit (the plan's note).
