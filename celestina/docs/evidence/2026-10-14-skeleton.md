```sh
cargo fmt --all --check
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --locked -- -D warnings
cargo test --workspace --locked
cargo build --release --locked
```

From the worktree root:

```sh
sh celestina/scripts/smoke.sh --binary <target>/release/celestina
bash scripts/check-architecture-contract.sh
python3 scripts/check-language-contract.py
```

By hand, in a nest (`dev-session.sh --own-bus --monitor none --once`), a

screenshot at rest, ran `celestina island open activity`, took a second
screenshot, then ran `island close`, `island toggle` and `quit`.

## Limits

- The host session's outputs were idle (the run was at night): the nested
  window received the host's throttled frame callbacks, about one per second,
  so the smoke saw 3 frames and the open growth was drawn in one or two
  frames. The 350 ms growth at 165 Hz, the blur over real windows and sharp
  text at both scales are the author's rows VAL-CEL-ISLAND, VAL-CEL-GLASS and
  VAL-CEL-SCALE.
- The nest has one output, `winit`; two-output behaviour (an output unplugged
  while a page is open) is covered by the runtime's `remove_output` order test
  and the app's `a_removed_output_drops_its_island_and_keeps_the_others`, not
  by a live run.
- `rest_frames` counts idle frames from one second after a surface's first
  frame. The clock's minute tick is such a frame, so the smoke starts away
  from a minute boundary; a niri model that reached the shell after that
  second without changing any dot would also count.
- `build-production.sh`, `verify-production.sh` and the deploy were not run:
  the landing seals and deploys.
