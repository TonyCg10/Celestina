## What was built

- **Pure domain (`celestina-shell`).**
  - `nightlight.rs` (9 tests) and `session.rs` (4 tests, trimmed to `Switch`
    and `PowerAction`) are copies by salvage; their head comments record the
    source path and commit.
  - `idle.rs` (16 tests) is new: the idle chain as a state machine with two
    steps, screen off and lock-and-suspend, with bounded delays (defaults 20 s
    and 240 s).
  - `settings.rs` gained `duties` (`idle`, `caffeine`, `nightlight`, each
    `"on"` or off; any other value reads as off) and `energy`
    (`caffeine`, `night_light`, `night_light_kelvin` clamped to 2000..=6500,
    default 2700). A 1.0.0 file reads with everything off; `schema_version` is
    unchanged.
- **Workers (`celestina/src/energy/`).**
  - `idle.rs` (14 tests): its own Wayland connection, `ext_idle_notifier_v1`
    on the seat, two notifications fed to the chain. Screen off goes through
    niri's bounded request queue, never a `niri msg` process. A missing global
    reports `unavailable` and does nothing.
  - `nightlight.rs` (18 tests): its own Wayland connection and
    `zwlr_gamma_control_manager_v1`, one controller per output only while the
    effect is on, the ramp over a `memfd`, transition frames, a backoff from
    1 s doubling to 30 s, neutral on any error, and teardown on switch-off,
    hot-unplug and exit.
  - `logind.rs` (15 tests): one worker on a blocking `zbus` connection to the
    system bus. Caffeine is `Inhibit("idle:sleep", ...)` in mode `block`;
    the returned descriptor is the lock and closing it is the release. Reboot,
    power off and suspend are `Manager` calls with `interactive = false`, each
    after its `CanXxx` probe. Every call has a timeout, the queue holds four
    requests, and text from the bus is bounded.
  - `verbs.rs` (6 tests) and `mod.rs` (10 tests): the `EnergyHandle` that
    starts a worker only for a duty that is `on`, is driven live by the one `shell.json` follower
    CEL-2-E landed (the same followed value feeds the wallpaper and the energy
    duties; this unit's own poll worker was dropped when it rebased on it)
    (a duty turning on starts its worker, turning off joins it and releases
    what it held), and the verbs `caffeine on|off|toggle`,
    `nightlight on|off|toggle` and `session suspend|reboot|power-off|log-out`.
    Caffeine and night-light verbs flip the persisted switch (durable before
    published) and do nothing, with a journal line, while their duty is off.
    Session verbs need no duty. The verbs print nothing to the client.

## Decisions as they ended

- **D1 — idle's lock timer.** The second step fails closed. `locker_available`
  is `false` until CEL-2-H, so lock-and-suspend is refused with a journal line
  and nothing suspends.
- **D2 — explicit `session suspend`.** Same rule: refused with "no locker yet"
  before any bus call. The plan left the question open for the author (§9); the
  implementation took the recommended answer.
- **D3 — gamma with several outputs.** One worker, a controller per output,
  rebuilt with backoff, destroyed on off, unplug and exit; a controller the
  compositor refuses leaves that output neutral and the duty reports "not
  applied".
- **D4 — persistence.** `energy.night_light`, `energy.night_light_kelvin` and
  `energy.caffeine` live in `shell.json` with the durable-before-published
  rule; the duty decides whether the shell acts on them.
- **D5 — compositor actions.** Screen off and log out go through the niri
  provider's request queue. Reboot, power off and suspend are logind calls.
  Log out is refused by the logind worker, which is not where it belongs.
- **D6 — caffeine is a descriptor,** not a child process: no `systemd-inhibit`.
- **D7 — `pending.rs` is not copied;** session verbs have a typed outcome.

## Finding: the system bus, and why the smoke never sends reboot

`logind.rs` connects with `LogindBus::System`, which is `zbus`'s system bus; the
nest's private session bus does not carry logind, and the nest does not
replace the system bus. Run unguarded in a nest, `caffeine` would take a real
`idle:sleep` inhibitor on the machine's logind, and `session reboot` would
call the real `CanReboot` and then `Reboot` with `interactive = false`. The
smoke therefore (a) never sends `session reboot` or `power-off`, (b) sends
`session suspend`, which the worker refuses before any bus call, and (c)
exports `DBUS_SYSTEM_BUS_ADDRESS` as a socket that does not exist for the
shell it starts, so caffeine can only report logind unavailable. Point (c)
rests on `zbus` honouring that variable, which the smoke checks only
indirectly (it fails on `caffeine: held`). Any later nested test of a power
verb must keep the same guard.

## Limits

Nothing was checked in a real session. Not proven:

- The real Wayland transports of gamma control and idle notification against a
  compositor: the unit tests drive the workers through fakes, and the nested
  smoke can bind at most the nest's globals.
- A real logind: the logind tests run against a fake `Manager` on a private
  bus; no real `Inhibit`, `Suspend`, `Reboot` or `PowerOff` was issued.
- DRM gamma on the three physical outputs, including the transition's
  appearance and hotplug.
- Live behaviour beside Noctalia: whether it and the shell fight over gamma,
  the inhibitor or idle when both are on (the VAL-CEL2 rows tell the author to
  switch the matching Noctalia duty off first).
- The verbs return no text to the client and no non-zero exit for a refusal;
  the result is in the journal and in `systemd-inhibit --list`. There is no
  `idle` verb (idle is a duty switch only), and `copy.rs` gained no strings.
- A retry happens only on the next `apply` (the next settings change): a
  command a worker refused is sent again then, not on a timer.
- `shell.json` echo race: two verbs inside the follower's coalescing window can make
  the shell re-apply an intermediate value, which the second write corrects
  (low). Overlapping writes by two processes can lose an update (last writer
  wins).
- A hostile peer of the same uid on the session bus can call `session reboot`
  or `session power-off`. That is not an escalation (the same uid can call
  logind directly); an optional hardening is to check `GetConnectionUnixUser`
  of the caller.
- The workers never reconnect after a lost Wayland connection: the night light
  stops and idle fails open (nothing sleeps or dims). Restarting the shell
  recovers.
- Two shells on a machine with no session bus would both run their duties; the
  single-instance guard is the session bus name.
- The nested smoke now gives every run (the first two included) a throwaway
  `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` and a system bus address that does not exist, so it never
  acts through the author's settings or logind. A worker left behind after
  2 s on a duty switched off and on again is the one overlap of two gamma
  clients, logged and recovered by the output backoff.
- The idle timings on the OLED, and the suspend step, which cannot run
  until a locker exists.

## Open risks

- `celestina-rs/crates/celestina-shell/src/nightlight.rs` keeps two
  `#[allow]` attributes inherited unchanged from the halted source: a
  `non_snake_case` on the `TEMPERATURE` binding and a block of
  `clippy::cast_*` on the ramp's `u16` conversion, the latter with a stated
  reason. They are salvage, copied byte for byte; removing them is an edit of
  the copy that this unit did not make.
- The night light and idle workers each hold a Wayland connection of their
  own, besides the runtime's.
- Duty switches live in `shell.json`; CEL-2-A also extends `duties` (a `bar`
  field), so whichever unit lands second merges that one struct by hand.
- The root `AGENTS.md` salvage list does not yet name the new copies; the
  author's edit to that section precedes this unit's commit.
